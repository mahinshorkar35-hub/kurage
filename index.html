Light as a physical material

Approach

Editorial. Slow. We work with glass plate, water column, optical film. Document how light behaves at each interface. Every project begins with a physical study.

Practice

Photography, writing, installation. Published work spans marine biology archives, architectural glass studies, and atmospheric documentation of urban light at dusk.
